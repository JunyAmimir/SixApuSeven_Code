# Continuous campus experience

Sign in as APU staff and choose Campus simulation from Data mode. It starts the morning rush automatically. Explore Demo operations and Demo student ride using the sidebar; the same clock, queues and backup actions continue in both views.

Students request rides, travel to check-in, enter the queue, and board every scheduled departure. Some requests expire without check-in. Adjust Student arrivals between quiet, typical and rush-hour traffic. Speed controls how many simulated minutes pass per second. Crowd figures are a visual sample, with exact queue totals written above them. All generated identities and activity are simulated.

Pause explicitly stops the clock. Skip to next event preserves the current running/paused state. Dispatch from a stop card to watch a backup travel, arrive and board at departure. The activity feed is bounded to the latest eight entries. Totals count activity since starting the experience; existing students from the opening morning queue can also board.

Return to live exits simulation and pauses it. Re-enter Campus simulation to resume that experience. Restart campus experience begins a new morning. Browser refresh starts the regular live workspace; in-memory simulated activity is not saved between refreshes. Service ends at 22:00; restart to demonstrate another morning.

Fixed walkthrough remains a separate, intentionally paused scenario with frozen new arrivals for exact before/after comparisons. It does not represent the continuous campus experience or real-world measured impact.


## Shared driver and disruptions

In continuous Campus simulation, staff request a backup from Demo operations. Open Demo driver hub and accept or decline it. Only accepted requests send a vehicle and add seats to its scheduled boarding. Duplicate active requests are blocked. Demo student ride shows the same clock, queues and service updates. Fixed walkthrough retains its original immediate dispatch so its repeatable comparison remains intact.

Use Event at to choose a stop. Lecture dismissal adds exactly 60 simulated ride requests, which check in over the following minutes. Delay moves the next regular bus five minutes later. Closure holds boarding for ten minutes while students continue requesting/checking in; the queue is preserved and the next available departure resumes boarding. The stop card and student departure estimate reflect disruptions. Driver acceptance at a closed stop is blocked; a previously accepted vehicle may wait for a later departure.

The visible map uses modeled positions, including accepted backup travel, not GPS. Session results count actual boarding, backup seats occupied and average check-in-to-boarding waiting time for passengers who boarded. People still waiting are shown separately and excluded from the served-passenger average. These are simulation metrics, not real-world impact or counterfactual savings.
